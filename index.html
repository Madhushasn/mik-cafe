About Mik Café

A neighbourhood café, run by family.

Mik Café is a family-owned specialty coffee and matcha café in Etobicoke, Ontario.

We believe a café should feel like an extension of the neighbourhood: somewhere you’re greeted warmly, handed something made with care, and always welcome to stay a while.

From handcrafted coffee beverages and specialty matcha drinks to fresh pastries and sandwiches, everything we do is about bringing people together — students with their books, remote workers with their laptops, and local residents catching up with friends.
